2. METHODOLOGY
   a. Exploratory Data Analysis — distribution plots, bar charts,
      correlation heatmap.
   b. Preprocessing — StandardScaler on all numeric features.
   c. Optimal k selection — Elbow (inertia), Silhouette, Davies-Bouldin,
      and Calinski-Harabasz scores evaluated for k in [2, 10].
   d. K-Means Clustering (primary model).
   e. Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering (validation / comparison).
   f. PCA (2-D) for visual validation of cluster separation.
   g. Segment profiling — mean numeric stats + modal categorical values.
   h. RFM analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) scatter.

7. MARKETING RECOMMENDATIONS
• High-income, high-spend segments → premium loyalty programs, early-access
  offers, concierge service.
• Budget shoppers → flash sales, value bundles, cashback campaigns.
• Low-recency customers (haven't visited recently) → win-back email/SMS
  campaigns with exclusive discounts.
• High online_purchase_pct segments → targeted digital ads, push
  notifications, and mobile-first UX improvements.
• Multi-category buyers → cross-sell recommendations and subscription
  boxes tailored to their purchase history.
